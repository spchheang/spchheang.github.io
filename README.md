I am Sreyphea Chheang, a postdoctoral fellow at CERDI, Université Clermont Auvergne. My GitHub username is **spchheang**.

I am a macroeconomist with research interests in economic growth and development, demographic economics, economic history, applied microeconomics, agricultural productivity, and development economics.

## Research

My research examines the long-term demographic and economic effects of historical disruptions and agricultural practices in Cambodia.

I am currently working on the ECOTERM project, which studies ecosystem services derived from termite mounds in the Lower Mekong Basin.

## Research Interests

- Economic Growth and Development
- Demographic Economics
- Economic History
- Applied Microeconomics
- Agricultural Productivity
- Development Economics
